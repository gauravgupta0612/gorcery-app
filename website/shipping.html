Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

  • We deliver groceries only within our service areas listed below; the app checks your pincode before you order.
  • Typical delivery time is 20-60 minutes after the order is confirmed, between 6:00 AM - 11:30 PM, all days. Orders placed outside these hours are delivered the next morning.
  • Delivery fee: ₹25 for orders below ₹299; free above ₹299. Minimum order ₹99.
  • A delivery OTP is shown in the app; please share it with the delivery partner to receive your order.
  • We do not ship outside India or by courier.
