Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Cancellation

  • You can cancel an order free of charge in the app until it is packed (status Placed or Confirmed).
  • After the order is packed or out for delivery it cannot be cancelled; you may refuse perishable items at the door if they are damaged.
  • We may cancel orders for stock or delivery reasons; you will be informed and refunded in full.

Returns and replacements

  • Damaged, expired, wrong or missing items: report within 24 hours of delivery from the order screen or by email/phone with a photo. We will replace the item or refund it.
  • Fresh fruits, vegetables, dairy and bakery items can be returned only at the time of delivery.
  • Opened personal care, baby care and food items cannot be returned unless defective.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds for online payments go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank) through Razorpay within 5-7 working days.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are made by UPI/bank transfer within 5-7 working days after you share your details.
  • If money was debited but the order failed, it is refunded automatically within 5-7 working days.
