Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how Gola Grocery ("we") collects and uses your information when you use our app and website.

Information we collect

  • Name, mobile number, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash) when you register.
  • Delivery addresses and, if you allow it, your location to place the delivery pin.
  • Order history, cart, wishlist, reviews and support messages.
  • Payment status and transaction IDs. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details - payments are handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy and PCI-DSS standards.
  • Device token for order notifications.

How we use it

  • To create your account, verify it with a one-time password (OTP) sent by email, process and deliver orders and process refunds.
  • To send order updates and, only if you agree, offers.
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal and tax obligations.

Sharing

We share only what is needed: your name, phone and address with our delivery partner for your order; payment details with Razorpay; and data with authorities when required by law. We do not sell your data.

Storage and security

Data is stored on secured cloud servers with encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited to authorised staff.

Your choices

You can view and edit your profile and addresses in the app. To delete your account and data, email ggupta865@gmail.com; we will do so within 30 days except records we must keep by law (e.g. invoices).
